🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 — Your digital privacy and safety are our foremost priority.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data: PlayNova does not require visitors to register, sign up, or create an account. We do not harvest, store, or sell personal identifiers such as your real name, physical address, financial card data, or telephone number.

Client-Side Local Storage: Game preferences, high scores, NovaWallet virtual coin balances, and level progress are persisted solely within your browser's local sandbox storage (`localStorage`). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to third parties.

2. Cookies & Analytics

We do not operate intrusive fingerprinting trackers. Any future analytical metrics or third-party ad serving (such as Google AdSense) are strictly configured under privacy-first standards, abiding by COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR compliance.

3. Children's Online Privacy Protection

PlayNova is dedicated to providing an inclusive, safe, and positive gaming space for minors and children. Our curated games contain no graphic violence, adult imagery, or deceptive monetization mechanics.

4. Data Deletion & Profile Reset

You maintain 100% control over your data. To completely wipe your game high scores, saved ratings, and NovaCoin balances at any time, simply clear your browser's site cookies and local storage for this domain.

5. Policy Updates & Contact

We may periodically update this policy to reflect platform improvements. For privacy-related questions, contact us via email at khairul2052007@gmail.com.
